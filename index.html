Ishita Gupta

I'm a second-year Master's student in Robotic Systems Development (MRSD) at Carnegie Mellon University, specializing in robotics and deep learning. Previously, I spent 2.5 years at Addverb as a Robotics Engineer, where I developed autonomous navigation systems, built physics-based simulators, and created multi-robot applications. I also interned at Google, automating cloud infrastructure pipelines for the Nest Devices team.

My research lies at the intersection of AI and robotics, with a focus on building physically intelligent systems: robots that can perceive, reason, and act in complex, dynamic environments. I'm particularly interested in humanoid robotics, embodied intelligence, and reinforcement learning, and in advancing how intelligent systems can move, adapt, and interact with the world in human-like ways.

Training Language Models to Self-Correct via Reinforcement Learning
CMU Course Project, 2024

project report

Led benchmarking of self-correction in LLMs, evaluating performance across Llama 3.2 1B, Llama 3.1 8B, and Mathstral 7B on MATH dataset. Achieved accuracy rates of up to 41.8%, identifying high Cor->Inc rates (46.9%) and low Inc->Cor improvement (3.78%). Engineered a multi-turn reinforcement learning framework (SCoRe) for fine-tuning.
